Crumple Camera

Pick a camera. Touch the picture and it peels off the screen like a sheet of paper. Push, drag and pinch it to crumple it. Hold still, then swipe it off the screen to keep it as a photo. Crumple it all the way and it turns into a paper ball and is thrown away. The video never leaves this device.
